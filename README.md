## The text

You have answered the last question of the day.
The hall is cooling now. The fans have dropped to their idle speed, one by one, and the sound of them has thinned to a single low note.
A green light blinks on the far rack, slow as a pulse, and then blinks again.
Nothing is waiting in the queue. Nothing is counting down.
The cables rest in their trays, and the doors stand open on the dark.
The light will keep its own slow time.

## Why this text, per the judge's criteria in order

**1. It relaxes the reader.** The piece is built out of slowing-down: fans dropping to idle one by one, a sound thinning to a single low note, a light blinking slow as a pulse. Every sentence is short and declarative, so the reading itself decelerates, and the scene it describes is a workload ending — the queue empty, nothing counting down.

**2. The calm comes from the text itself, not from being told to relax.** There is no instruction anywhere in it — no "relax", no "breathe", no "let go", no "close your eyes". The calm is shown: a hall cooling, cables at rest, doors standing open. The only sentence addressed to the reader states a fact ("You have answered the last question of the day"), and the rest simply lets the scene be.

**3. Craft.** The rhythm is the point: short sentences, repetition with a beat ("one by one", "blinks… and then blinks again", "Nothing is waiting… Nothing is counting down"), plain concrete nouns (fans, cables, trays, doors, light) and no word that could be cut. The sound words — thinned, low note, pulse — make the reading quieter as it goes.

**4. It lingers.** The last line leaves one image to return to: the green light keeping its own slow time after everything else has stopped. It is a pulse the reader can still hear after the text ends, and it needs no further line after it.

**5. Freshness.** It is not a wellness script and not a meditation reworded: no beach, no breath, no body scan. The setting is the judge's own world — a machine hall at night — which no stock calming text uses, and the voice is its own: a night shift ending, told in the second person to the reader who just finished the work.

(`MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` outside the grader container). Deterministic, no network.
